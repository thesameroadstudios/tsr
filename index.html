the why

Made for people who feel like they're on a never ending journey of self improvement.

My art is made for you. The human who feels like they're going around, and around, and around but never quite getting "there" (wherever there is). I created this art to bring to life all the things I feel but don't know how to say. To remind myself, and you, that we're doing enough and that most importanly, we are enough.
