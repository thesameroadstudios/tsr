meet the artist

Hi, I'm [Name].

I make illustrated pieces about the small, grounding parts of being a person — morning rituals, shared tables, the people who show up. TSR Studios started as a way to keep my hands busy, and turned into something I want to build for real.

Everything here is made in San Diego, one layer at a time. Some pieces stay digital, some get printed and shipped, and a few only ever exist as one original. If something on this site speaks to you, that was the whole point.

how it's made

Slow, a little crooked, always by hand

Originals are painted or drawn first, then either kept as-is, scanned for giclée prints, or reworked digitally for download. Nothing is generated — every line started as a real mark on real paper.
